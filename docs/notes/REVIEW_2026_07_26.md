## 1. CRITICAL - coarse-device energy is silently dropped (breaks "accuracy is the product")

**Where:** `engine/accountant.py` (`_spread_device` → `_is_finalised`) with
`_DEFAULT_LATENESS = 15 min`; encoded in
`test_a_delta_for_an_already_finalised_bucket_is_dropped`; understated in
PLAN.md ("Energy totals stay correct") and HEA-48 (filed as **Low**, framed as
cost mis-attribution only).

**The defect:** a delta's portions that fall into buckets at or before the
watermark (≈ now − 20 min) are discarded. The cycle-resetting aircons - the founding use
case - report in 0.25 kWh steps every **15-90+ minutes**. Every delta whose
span exceeds ~20 minutes loses its older portion:

- 22-min span → ~23% of that delta dropped
- 37-min span → ~46% dropped
- 90-min span → ~78% dropped

Real capture (a coarse-step aircon, Jul 8): consecutive steps at 21/22/31/37/44-minute
gaps. Expected systematic undercount for the aircons: roughly **30-50% of their
energy**, silently reattributed to Untracked (the aggregate invariant still
holds, which is why nothing looks obviously broken). The accountant docstring's
"the amounts are tiny" claim is true for finely-updating meters and false for
exactly the devices this product was built around. Cloud-polled Tuya devices
are affected the same way.

**Why it wasn't caught:** engine tests feed readings and finalize far in the
future (no progressive finalization); the golden-master deliberately scopes to
delta extraction, not the runtime; and HEA-28 (the one-week reconciliation
validation) has not run yet.

**Proposed fix (detailed):**

*Primary - retained-context reallocation.* Keep each finalised bucket's context
(source energies, prices, draws, and the allocation it produced) in a bounded
ring (e.g. 3 h ≈ 36 buckets; configurable). When a late device portion arrives
for a retained bucket: re-run the allocation for that bucket with the added
draw, and apply the *difference* to the running totals (device gains, Untracked
gives back). Invariant preserved exactly; totals stay live (15-min lateness
kept); memory is trivial. Portions older than the ring are then genuinely
negligible and may be dropped - with a `Decision` log entry (`DROPPED_LATE`)
so diagnostics can prove it.

*Secondary (cheap, complementary):* also subscribe the coordinator to
`state_reported` events (`async_track_state_report_event`). Polled integrations
(the aircon integration polls ~1/min) then refresh `_last` on every unchanged report, shrinking
delta spans - fewer late portions in the first place. Trade-off to note in the
ADR: with short spans, a 0.25 kWh step concentrates into the final bucket
instead of spreading over the true accrual window; the retained ring makes this
tolerable rather than load-bearing.

*Also:* update ADR-0005 (or a new ADR-0006) to own the lateness/late-arrival
policy explicitly - it is currently asserted in a docstring, not decided in an
ADR; correct PLAN.md's "energy totals stay correct" claim; re-scope HEA-48 from
Low to **Urgent** with this framing; add an engine test that finalises
*progressively* (tick-style) over the real coarse-step cadence and asserts
zero energy loss.

**Gate for release:** HEA-28's reconciliation week should not start until this
lands - it would currently "validate" an undercount.

## 2. HIGH - house reconfigure wipes the helper-ownership bookkeeping

`config_flow.async_step_reconfigure` calls
`async_update_reload_and_abort(entry, data=user_input)` - wholesale replacement
of `entry.data`. But `CONF_INTEGRAL_HELPERS` and `CONF_CYCLE_METERS` live in
`entry.data` too, so reconfiguring the house drops the ownership maps. The
sync modules mostly re-adopt by source-matching, so it self-heals *silently* -
but between reconfigure and next reconcile, removal paths can orphan or
mis-delete helpers. **Fix:** merge - `data={**entry.data, **user_input}` (and a
regression test that reconfigure preserves bookkeeping keys). Consider moving
bookkeeping out of user-facing data entirely (entry.options namespace or a
`Store`) as the structural fix.

## 3. HIGH - adopted pre-existing helpers can be deleted as if HEA owned them

`_integral_helper_for_source` / `_utility_meter_for` match **any** existing
helper over the source - including one the *user* created before installing
HEA (entirely plausible: a Riemann helper on a wall-light power sensor).
Reuse is right; but the entry-id goes into the owned map, and device removal /
`async_remove_entry` then **deletes the user's helper**. **Fix:** only delete
helpers HEA created. Track provenance: when `_…_for()` finds an existing entry
not already in the owned map, record it as `adopted` (never delete), vs
`created` (delete on cleanup). Small data-model change to both sync modules +
tests for the adopted-then-removed path.

## 4. MEDIUM - unbounded growth and restart losses in the runtime

- **`Accountant._prices` grows forever** and `_price_at` scans linearly from
  index 0 - months of a 6-change/day tariff is fine, but a spot-price tariff
  (Nordpool: 24-96 changes/day) reaches tens of thousands of entries, scanned
  per finalized bucket. **Fix:** prune on `finalize()` - drop entries strictly
  older than the last applicable one before the watermark; optionally bisect.
- **Every reload discards in-flight accounting.** Unfinalized buckets (up to
  ~20 min × all devices) die with the coordinator on restart *and on every
  options/config change* (the update listener reloads the entry). **Fix:** on
  unload, flush - `finalize(now + lateness + BUCKET)` and publish once - so
  sensors bank the totals into their restore baseline before teardown. One
  method + one test.
- **Cold-start pricing:** buckets finalized before the first price reading are
  costed at 0 forever (tested, deliberate). With the retained-ring fix (#1),
  re-price them when the first price arrives; until then, at minimum log a
  decision so diagnostics explain it.

## 5. MEDIUM - source-sensor validation gap in the flows

Device selection filters `device_class` only. A `total` (net) counter, a
forecast kWh sensor (no `state_class`), or a decreasing sensor can be selected;
the engine reads decreases as resets → phantom energy. Discovery even offers
forecast sensors (sorted last, but selectable). ADR-0004 scopes these out of
MVP - but the boundary is not enforced anywhere. **Fix:** validate the chosen
sensor's `state_class == total_increasing` (energy path) / `measurement`
(power path) in `_validate_device_sources` and discovery's candidate filter;
reject with a translated error. Cheap, closes a real footgun for external
users.

## Addendum (2026-07-26) - Untracked correction semantics and ring depth

Two challenges the maintainer raised against §1's fix, both valid; HEA-48 was revised
accordingly.

**Untracked cannot dip under `total_increasing`.** Late-arrival corrections
move value out of Untracked, but its Energy Used / Actual Cost / Cost Without
Solar sensors are `total_increasing` - HA statistics read a dip as a meter
reset and corrupt long-term statistics. Resolution: (a) Untracked's sensors
move to `state_class: total` (legitimately decreasing, like Cost Savings; the
cycle meters already set `net_consumption` for TOTAL sources); (b) rule:
**corrections never reduce a tracked device** - under proportional allocation
the blended unit price is unchanged by a late arrival, so value moves only
from Untracked to the late device; in over-drawn buckets the late gain is
capped at what Untracked holds. (c) the maintainer's derive-on-the-fly proposal was
checked and is *simpler*, not harder: Untracked ≡ house totals − Σ device
totals holds identically under the shared blended price, so the engine can
track house + devices and derive the remainder - consistent by construction,
and a monotonic Whole-home total falls out for free. Derivation is the
preferred implementation, with accumulated-with-corrections as the acceptable
fallback if restore-baseline snapshotting proves awkward; (a) and (b) are firm
either way.

**Is the ring just a longer window?** Partly - but with live publishing at
the 15-min lateness and *exact* retro-correction using each bucket's retained
prices, unlike a raised margin which delays everything. And the window can be
generous: retained context is a few KB per bucket, so the default is now
**24 h (<1 MB)**, covering even twice-a-day counters. With the
`state_reported` subscription, polled integrations keep spans at their poll
interval regardless of how rarely the counter steps - ring depth only matters
for push-only sources that emit nothing between rare changes, and residual
loss beyond the ring is logged (`DROPPED_LATE`), never silent.
